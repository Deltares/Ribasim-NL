add_manning_nodes_between_splitted_basins Add ManningResistance nodes between split basin polygons that share a boundary face. A ManningResistance node is added at the centroid of the shared boundary face and connected to the two basins with links. This ensures that water can flow between the newly created basins. They are added on each face. This may be redundant, but this will improve recognizability of the model and is not expected to cause performance issues, since the number of basins that are split and the number of shared faces is expected to be low.

Split a Basin in sections along route, connected in the direction of route by ManningResistances.

route runs through the Basin area in the direction of flow, for instance along the links of its main channel. The ManningResistances lie on it at the points nearest to split_points, where the Basin area is cut across it. The section that contains the Basin node keeps it; the others get a new Basin node on the route, halfway between where their inflows and outflows join it, so their links do not run back along the route. Each section gets the profile of the Basin with its area scaled by the share of the section in the length of the route within the Basin, so the volume is divided in proportion, and copies of the other tables of the Basin. The flow links of the Basin, which keep their IDs, move to the section where they join the route, and run along the route to its node. The ManningResistances get manning_n, profile_slope, the width of the Basin area across the route, and the length of their links, which run along the route from Basin node to Basin node.

Parameters
