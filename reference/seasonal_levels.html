seasonal_levels

seasonal_levels

Summer and winter target levels (zomer- en winterpeil) in a model with one target level per Basin.

The models are built with one target level per Basin, meta_streefpeil in the Basin / area table, from which the levels of their control are derived: the thresholds of the DiscreteControl conditions on its level, and the min_upstream_level and max_downstream_level of the Outlets and Pumps around it. apply_seasonal_levels varies these over the year with the level management of the peilgebieden of ribasim_nl.peilbeheer, see https://github.com/Deltares/Ribasim-NL/issues/893:

  1. assign_peilbeheer gives a Basin the level management of the peilgebied that covers most of its area, or of the weir near it with the closest target level. Its target level stays the same in the season of which the target level of the peilgebied is closest to it, since the target levels of the models are checked with the water boards. The peilgebied gives the difference with the other season, and the bounds of the target level. A peilgebied of which both target levels are far from that of the Basin does not describe its water, and is not assigned. join_peilbeheer gives the Basins that are joined by resistance nodes, and so are one water in the model, the level management of the largest area of them, also none.
  2. The thresholds of the DiscreteControl conditions on the level of a Basin follow its target level. Thresholds with the default band of DEFAULT_LEVEL_THRESHOLD_RANGE around a level get the bounds of the target level instead.
  3. The Outlets and Pumps controlled by a DiscreteControl node get a winter and a summer variant of each control state, with the levels of that season. A condition on the season switches between them.
  4. The initial level of a Basin follows its target level at the start of the model.

The levels change at once, at the begin of the transitions between summer and winter. A gradual transition needs Ribasim to support parameters of control states that change over time. The DiscreteControl nodes that change over the year get cyclic timeseries, over the year of the start of the model.

The regional models get the seasons in ribasim_nl.regional_model.finish_regional_model. koppelen removes them with remove_seasonal_levels, since the coupling works with the fixed levels, and applies them again to the coupled model. The fixed levels and thresholds are kept in the meta_fixed_ columns for this.

assign_peilbeheer

seasonal_levels.assign_peilbeheer(
    model,
    peilgebieden,
    authority=None,
    min_overlap=MIN_OVERLAP,
    max_offset=MAX_OFFSET,
)

Assign the level management of the peilgebieden to the Basins with a target level.

A Basin gets the peilgebied of its authority that covers most of its area, if that is at least min_overlap of it. A Basin without such a peilgebied gets the peilgebied that is a point within POINT_DISTANCE of it with the closest target level, such as the weir that it drains over. The levels of the peilgebied are shifted such that its target level in the season of which it is closest to the target level of the Basin, is that target level. A peilgebied of which both target levels are further than max_offset from that of the Basin does not describe its water, and is not assigned.

Parameters

Name Type Description Default
model Model model required
peilgebieden gpd.GeoDataFrame the level management of the peilgebieden, see ribasim_nl.peilbeheer.read_peilbeheer required
authority str | None the authority of all Basins of a regional model, or else their authority follows from their node ID, as in the coupled model None
min_overlap float the minimum fraction of the area of a Basin that its peilgebied covers MIN_OVERLAP
max_offset float the maximum difference between the target levels of the Basin and its peilgebied [m] MAX_OFFSET

Returns

join_peilbeheer

seasonal_levels.join_peilbeheer(model, regimes)

Give the Basins that are joined by resistance nodes the same level management.

The flow between these Basins follows their difference in level, so they are one water in the model, of which the seasons should not change the levels relative to each other. Of their level management, also none, the one of the largest area applies to all of them, relative to their own target level.

Parameters

Name Type Description Default
model Model model required
regimes pd.DataFrame the level management of the Basins, see assign_peilbeheer required

Returns

Name Type Description
pd.DataFrame the level management of the Basins, see assign_peilbeheer, with overlap and offset NaN for the Basins that get it from the Basins they are joined to

season_levels

seasonal_levels.season_levels(static, neighbors, differences, bottoms)

The min_upstream_level and max_downstream_level of static rows in each season.

The finite levels move with the target level of the Basin upstream and downstream of their node respectively. Ribasim requires a min_upstream_level above the bottom of the Basin upstream, so it stays above it.

Parameters

Name Type Description Default
static pd.DataFrame static rows of Outlets or Pumps required
neighbors pd.DataFrame the upstream and downstream node per node_id required
differences pd.DataFrame see seasonal_differences required
bottoms pd.Series the lowest level of the profile per Basin required

Returns

Name Type Description
pd.DataFrame the levels with the suffix _zomer and _winter, with the index of static

season_switch

seasonal_levels.season_switch(transitions, year)

The season over a year, indexed by the time from which it applies.

It is 1 in summer, from the begin of the transition to summer until the begin of the transition to winter, and 0 in winter.
