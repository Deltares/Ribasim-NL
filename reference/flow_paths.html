flow_paths

flow_paths

Routes of flow links through Junctions, and changes to them that only change the layout.

A Junction only merges or splits flow, so the flow links from a node through Junctions up to the next non-Junction nodes form the route of that node. Ribasim replaces each pair of links into and out of a Junction by a direct link, see simplify_graph! in the Ribasim core. Moving or adding Junctions on a route therefore changes how links are drawn, not how water flows, as long as each node keeps exchanging water with the same non-Junction nodes.

Routes

flow_paths.Routes(model)

The routes of the flow links of a model through Junctions, as they are when the Routes are made.

Ribasim removes the Junctions without rows in Junction / static, the plain Junctions, before the simulation, see simplify_graph! in the Ribasim core. A connected group of plain Junctions, ignoring the direction of the links, with exactly one Basin as a neighbor, connects each other neighbor with that Basin, in the direction of its link with the group: a Basin tree. The direction of the links within a Basin tree, and with its Basin, does not matter. Other groups connect each node upstream of a Junction with each node downstream of it, along the links.

attach_to_tree

flow_paths.attach_to_tree(routes, start, points)

Attach points to the tree of routes from start, each with a straight line from the nearest point of the tree.

The points nearest to the tree are attached first, such that the others can branch off their lines. Where a line branches off a segment, a point is inserted in all routes along that segment, such that they share it.

Name Type Description

branch_from_route

flow_paths.branch_from_route(model, node_id, basin_id, towards_id, margin=1.0)

Let the link from basin_id into node_id branch off the route between basin_id and towards_id.

The branch starts at a Junction at the point of that route nearest to node_id, or at the existing Junction if that point is within margin of one, and runs straight to node_id. Returns the node id of that Junction.

drainage_capacity

flow_paths.drainage_capacity(model)

The flow that each node can pass in the drainage situation, to choose the direction of Basin tree links.

For Pumps and Outlets the largest flow_rate or max_flow_rate without control state, or in a control state with “afvoer”. ManningResistances, and placeholders and missing maxima, are unlimited (infinite). Also the LinearResistances, TabulatedRatingCurves, FlowBoundaries and UserDemands, by their largest flow or demand.

merge_basin_trees

flow_paths.merge_basin_trees(model, read_lines=None)

Give each Basin one Junction tree for its inflows and outflows, along the current routes of its links.

The routes of the links of a Basin are followed through the Junctions of its Basin trees, see Routes, up to the fixed_junctions, and joined where they share their coordinates from the Basin node on, see build_basin_tree. A Basin whose tree would not change is left as it is. Each node keeps exchanging water with the same nodes, which is asserted.

place_on_link

flow_paths.place_on_link(model, node_id, basin_id, link_id)

Move node_id onto the point of link_id nearest to it, with its link with basin_id along their route.

link_id is on the route of basin_id, see Routes.paths, and node_id has a single flow link with basin_id. Its other links move their ends along.

prune_junction

flow_paths.prune_junction(model, junction_id)

Remove a Junction without inflow or outflow, or merge the two links of a Junction that only passes flow on.

A Junction of a Basin tree, see Routes, only needs two links, in any direction. Neighboring Junctions are pruned in turn. A Junction with other links, such as observation links, is kept.

rebuild_junction_trees

flow_paths.rebuild_junction_trees(
    model,
    basin_ids,
    waterways,
    tolerance=10.0,
    water=None,
    routing_areas=None,
)

Route the flow links of Basins along the waterways, with one Junction tree per Basin, split where they split.

The Junctions of the Basin trees of a Basin, see junction_tree, are removed, except the fixed_junctions. The links to the nodes they reach are routed from the Basin node along the shortest paths of one network, see ribasim_nl.network_snapping.route_basin_links, such that its inflows and outflows share one tree, see build_basin_tree. With open water, such as a lake, the waterways are connected over it, and nodes far from them are routed over it, straight from the nearest point of the waterways from which that is possible. The others branch off straight from the nearest point of these routes, see attach_to_tree. Each node keeps exchanging water with the same nodes, which is asserted, and the links to the nodes reached keep their link ids. Nodes are not moved and links are not offset, see ribasim_nl.layout.tidy_layout.

Parameters

Name Type Description Default
model Model Ribasim model required
basin_ids typing.Iterable[int] Basins to rebuild the Junctions of required
waterways gpd.GeoDataFrame waterways with a boolean “main-route” column, see ribasim_nl.network_snapping.snap_to_network required
tolerance float buffer around the basin area within which waterways are used, defaults to 10.0 10.0
water gpd.GeoDataFrame | None open water, over which the waterways are connected and nodes far from them are routed to them, see ribasim_nl.network_snapping.route_basin_links, defaults to None. The waterways on it, such as fairways, are not digitized in the direction of the flow, so they do not set the direction of links, see towards_basin. None
routing_areas typing.Mapping[int, shapely.Geometry] | None per Basin, an area beyond its basin area in which the waterways are used as well, for connector nodes that are connected to it over a long distance, defaults to None None

Returns

Name Type Description
tuple[list[int], list[int]] the ids of the links and of the Junctions that are new or changed

Rebuild the Junctions of Rijkswaterstaat Basins along the waterways and a main route, see rebuild_junction_trees.

This is used for the hws model and again after coupling, such that its Basins keep their routes. towards_basin

flow_paths.towards_basin(inflows, outflows, capacity, digitized_away=None)

Whether a link of a Basin tree points towards its Basin, given the nodes beyond it that flow in and out.

The direction does not change the simulation, only the sign of the flow of the link in the results. A link that only carries inflows points towards the Basin, and one that only carries outflows away from it. A link that carries both follows the main flow: the direction in which the lines it follows are digitized, if they decide, else the side with the larger drainage_capacity, else the side with more nodes, else away from the Basin.

Parameters

Name Type Description Default
digitized_away bool | None whether the lines along the link are digitized away from the Basin, see Digitized.along None
