peilbeheer

peilbeheer

The level management (peilbeheer) of the peilgebieden of the water boards, in summer and winter.

The water boards deliver their peilgebieden in their own formats. read_peilbeheer reads them with the attribute mappings of SOURCES, see https://github.com/Deltares/Ribasim-NL/issues/893, into one table with per peilgebied the type of level management, the target level (streefpeil) and its lower and upper bound (ondergrens and bovengrens) in summer and winter, and the dates of the transitions between them. Where a dataset does not give these, they follow from the rules of complete_peilbeheer:

  • Without a target level, the target level is the mean of the bounds.
  • A season without a target level gets the levels of the other season, as for a fixed level.
  • Without a bound, or with a bound that does not lie within MAX_BOUND_DISTANCE beyond the target level, the bound is DEFAULT_BOUND from the target level, which is the band of the DiscreteControl thresholds of the model.
  • The transition from the winter to the summer level is from 1 to 10 April, and back from 1 to 10 October. Datasets with only the date at which the level changes start a transition of TRANSITION_DAYS days on that date. The models change their levels at once, at the begin of a transition, see ribasim_nl.seasonal_levels.

Levels of 99 m from NAP or more are unknown values, and so are levels of 0 in datasets that use 0 for unknown values, see valid_levels. Peilgebieden of which the summer and winter level differ more than MAX_SEASONAL_DIFFERENCE are data errors, and skipped. Most peilgebieden are areas, but they can also be points, such as weirs with their target levels.

A dataset with the peilgebieden of a water board, and the attributes of their levels.

mapping maps a value of attribute by to the attributes of the levels of the peilgebieden with that value, and "*" to those of all peilgebieden. The levels are streefpeil, ondergrens and bovengrens for both seasons, and these with the suffix _zomer or _winter for one season. Peilgebieden with other values of by are skipped.

valid_levels

peilbeheer.valid_levels(values, zero_is_unknown=False)

Return the levels as floats, with NaN for unknown levels.

Datasets use 99 or 9999 m NAP for unknown levels, so levels of 99 m from NAP or more are unknown. Some datasets also use 0, which is otherwise a level like any other.
