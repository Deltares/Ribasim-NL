result_validation

result_validation

Regression checks on the results of a model run, which supplement the input checks of ribasim_nl.validation.

result_metrics summarizes a run in a few numbers: solver statistics, how well the simulated discharges match the measurements of the Observation nodes, see ribasim_nl.goodness_of_fit, and the discharges and levels at key points of the main water system. validate_results checks them against LIMITS. The limits are loose bounds around the results of a run of lhm_coupled, so that they catch large unintended regressions rather than the noise of small changes. Change them when a change of the results is intended, and explain why in the pull request.
