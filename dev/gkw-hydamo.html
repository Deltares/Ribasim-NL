HyDAMO data from GKW

The input models of the gravity controlled water authorities are lumped from HyDAMO data: the hydroobjects, which are the watercourses, and the structures on them. Most of that data was delivered by the water authorities in 2023 and 2024. The data of Brabantse Delta now comes from the national GKW-HyDAMO package (#1075), and the other authorities can follow.

The package

The water authorities deliver their HyDAMO data to the Gegevensknooppunt waterschappen (GkW) of Het Waterschapshuis, which passes on the objects that pass its checks, see its dashboard. NHI publishes the result as one GeoPackage of all authorities, gkw-hydamo-package.zip.

  • It has no versions: the file is replaced in place. So DVC tracks a copy of its GeoPackage, data/Basisgegevens/GKW-HyDAMO/LHM_HyDAMO.gpkg, which all authorities share. The desc in its .dvc file gives the date on which it was published.
  • Codes are not unique nationally: the objects of an authority are those with a nen3610id that starts with NL.WBHCODE.<waterbeheercode>., see ribasim_nl.lumping.gkw_hydamo.read_authority.

Update the copy to the latest package with:

pixi run python scripts/download_gkw_hydamo.py
pixi run dvc add data/Basisgegevens/GKW-HyDAMO/LHM_HyDAMO.gpkg

This downloads the package, 1.8 GB, and unpacks its GeoPackage, 5.4 GB. Put the date that the script logs in the desc of LHM_HyDAMO.gpkg.dvc, which dvc add keeps. The stages gkw_hydamo of all authorities then run again.

From the package to the network

pipeline/00_gkw_hydamo.py selects the hydroobjects that the network is lumped from, with three lists per authority:

  • CATEGORIES: the categories of hydroobjects, primair for Brabantse Delta.
  • CODES: hydroobjects of other categories that the network needs as well. For Brabantse Delta these are 71 watercourses of the delivery of 2023 that are secundair or tertiair since, with a new code.
  • EXCLUDED: hydroobjects of these categories that are left out, see below.

The lumping connects hydroobjects only where their ends have the same coordinates. ribasim_nl.lumping.connect therefore makes the selected hydroobjects into lines that connect:

Step What it does Code of the result
join_parts Parts of a hydroobject that follow each other, delivered as a multi-part line or as objects with the same code, become one line The parts that are left get <code>.2, .3
snap_ends Ends within 1 m of each other get the same coordinate
split_at_junctions A line is split where another one ends on it within 1 m The pieces get <code>-0, -1, along the line

split_at_junctions is a port of the preprocessing of 2024, and gives the pieces the codes they got then. snap_ends is new: in the package, the ends of about 1100 hydroobjects of Brabantse Delta miss the end of their neighbour by less than a millimetre. Without it, these lines would all be extended to each other, and renamed <code>-0. The distance of 1 m, ribasim_nl.lumping.gkw_hydamo.CONNECT_DISTANCE, connects the 9 junctions of Brabantse Delta that miss each other by 0.5 to 0.95 m. A larger distance would also connect the watercourses on either side of a pumping station.

pipeline/00a_process_hydamo.py then removes the objects of hydamo_verwijderen.gpkg, by their code, adds those of hydamo_toevoegen.gpkg, and connects the hydroobjects again. So a hydroobject that is added by hand connects where it ends within 1 m of another one, which is split there. It fails if a removal no longer removes an object, or if two hydroobjects have the same code, as can happen when the data is updated.

What does not follow the data

  • Split nodes and boundaries. ribasim_input.gpkg holds their location, and the code and type of the structure of each split node, so the keys of the nodes do not change with the data. A split node that is further than 1 m from the hydroobjects is left out by the lumping, and one that no longer separates two basins is not used.
  • Open water split nodes. A split node on open water is named after its hydroobject. The split nodes that the lumping of 2024 named after the edge they were exactly on, such as OVK01598___1, have that name in ribasim_input.gpkg now: whether a point is exactly on a line depends on the last digits of the coordinates.
  • Peilgebieden. The peilgebieden of Brabantse Delta in the package have target levels without the type of level: summer, winter or fixed. They remain those of the delivery of 2023, 1_ontvangen_data/peilgebieden.gpkg.
  • Target levels. The Basins in parameters/static_data.xlsx keep the target level they have there, see StaticData.keep_streefpeil. A level that 02_prepare_model.py derives from the profiles follows the Basin node, which moves when the hydroobjects change: one Basin got a target level 5 cm lower, which lowered the level up to which the pumping stations that discharge into it may pump by 5 cm too, so that in the coupled model they stopped and the polders behind them filled up. A new Basin gets the derived level.

New hydroobjects that are left out

A hydroobject that is new in the package can change the model without anyone choosing that: the lumping makes one basin of all hydroobjects that are connected without a split node between them. Two kinds of new hydroobjects are therefore in EXCLUDED, until they get a split node of their own:

  • a hydroobject that bypasses a structure with a split node, such as a fish passage around a weir, which would leave that structure out of the model;
  • a hydroobject that connects two basins of the model without a structure between them, which would make them one basin.

Where a hydroobject now continues to a junction that it did not reach in 2023, which joins two basins, a split node of type harde_knip in ribasim_input.gpkg cuts it, as in the data of 2023. Where the watercourse of a structure itself is replaced, the model follows the data: the split node of a weir that is gone is removed, and its basins become one.

Update the data of an authority

For an update of the package, start at step 2. To move another authority to the package:

  1. Add the authority to the stage gkw_hydamo in dvc.yaml, to CATEGORIES in pipeline/00_gkw_hydamo.py, and to GKW_AUTHORITIES in pipeline/00a_process_hydamo.py. 2_voorbewerking/hydamo.gpkg is then an output of the stage, so remove its .dvc file. Which identifiers of the data of 2023 and 2024 are still in the package differs per authority, see #1075.
  2. Run the stages gkw_hydamo, hydamo and lumped of the authority.
  3. Compare the input model with the previous one, by key:
    • connector nodes that are gone: their split node is off the hydroobjects, or bypassed;
    • Basins that are gone, which are merged or named after another connector node, and Basins without outflow, where a watercourse is interrupted;
    • links that are reversed, where a hydroobject is drawn in the other direction.
  4. Decide per difference: move or remove the split node, exclude the new hydroobject, or add a hydroobject by hand where the data has a gap, for instance at a culvert.
  5. Refer to the nodes that got another key by that key in the scripts, model_edits.gpkg and static_data.xlsx of the authority. A key that is gone raises a KeyError where it is used.
  6. Keep the target levels of static_data.xlsx, with StaticData.keep_streefpeil in 02_prepare_model.py, and compare the Basins and the static tables of the Pumps and Outlets of the full control model with the previous one, by key.
